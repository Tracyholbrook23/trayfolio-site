Before you book

What changes the price

The size of the vehicle

A three row SUV has more glass, more carpet and more panel than a coupe. The Basic package is quoted as a range for exactly this reason.

The condition it arrives in

A car detailed every month is a different job to one that has not been touched in two years. Heavy soiling, staining and neglected paint all take longer.

Pet hair and the hard stuff

Embedded pet hair, sand, spills and smoke all add time to an interior. Send photos and it gets priced honestly up front rather than renegotiated in your driveway.

Get a real number

Text a few photos of the vehicle and where it is parked. You will get a price and a time back, usually the same day.
