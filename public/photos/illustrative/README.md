# Illustrative imagery, NOT portfolio

Everything in this folder is generated, not photographed. These are generic
"this is what clean looks like" detail shots, the same role a stock photo
plays on any service website.

The rule this folder exists to enforce:

- These images may be used in the SERVICES sections, where they illustrate
  what a service involves.
- They must NEVER appear in the "Recent work" gallery, in a before/after
  pair, or anywhere captioned as a job the business completed. That gallery
  is real client work only, and it is the thing a customer judges.

Every real photo lives in ../ (public/photos) and in ./interior for crops of
the one interior photo the client supplied. If more real interior photos come
in, swap them into the sequence and delete from here.

## ./coverage

The five package visualizer states on /pricing (basic, paint, interior, works,
ceramic), together with ../../video/works-to-interior.mp4. All generated.

These are diagrams of what each package touches, drawn photorealistically
rather than as line art, and the line under the stage on the page says so out
loud. The same rule applies as everything else in this folder: never in the
gallery, never in a before/after pair, never captioned as a completed job.

Every one of them is an edit of a single source plate,
_video-sources/pricing-states/plate-a.png, which is what keeps the car, the
camera, the paint and the background identical across all five states. If they
are ever regenerated, regenerate them from that plate rather than from scratch,
or they stop being the same car. docs/pricing-visualization-plan.md has the
prompts and the reasoning.
